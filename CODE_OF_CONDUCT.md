# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its full text applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, reviews and
every other place where the project is discussed.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good faith. People here write in English and
  in Russian, often as a second language; read generously.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing
  of someone's private information.
- Accept the maintainer's decisions on what is on topic and what gets
  merged, and disagree politely.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer, [@bropines](https://github.com/bropines), about
behaviour that breaks these rules. The project has no separate contact
address for this: use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature on the comment or issue in question, which also reaches GitHub's
own staff, or mention the maintainer in the thread if the matter can be
discussed in public. Reports are handled in confidence as far as the
channel allows.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock discussions,
and block people from the repository, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant: a correction first, then a
warning, a temporary ban and a permanent ban, according to how serious
and how repeated the behaviour is.
